# Goal celebration

`goal-stadium.mp3` is adapted from **Football-crowd-GOAL.wav** by **paulw2k**, published August 9, 2013 on Freesound. Recorded at Brentford versus Yeovil in September 2006, capturing a home-team goal.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/paulw2k/sounds/196461/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/196/196461_755728-hq.mp3
- Changes: extracted 4.4–11.4 seconds to start at the goal roar, loudness normalized to -16 LUFS with -2 dB true-peak target; 25 ms fade-in and 1.7-second fade-out; encoded at 160 kbps MP3.

Bundled locally; playback makes no third-party requests. Attribution is also available in the game's pause menu. The creator does not endorse this game.

# Referee whistle

`referee-whistle.mp3` is adapted from **referee-whistle.wav** by **Pablo-F**, published February 20, 2010 on Freesound. A recording of a football (soccer) referee whistle using a SAMSON C01 microphone.

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Pablo-F/sounds/90743/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Source preview: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/90/90743_1476665-hq.mp3
- Changes: loudness normalized to -20 LUFS with -3 dB true-peak target; encoded at 160 kbps MP3. Playback excerpts have short fades and are arranged into a single restart blast, a long-short foul call, or three full-time blasts with a longer final blast. No pitch shifting.

Bundled locally; playback makes no third-party requests. The creator does not endorse this game.
